Artist statement

About

I am self-trained and have little to no ability to depict anything accurately. I enjoy painting as a way to explore color combinations, depth, and movement through space.

I start with an idea of what I want to portray and apply a low to no judgment approach to what follows. I enjoy trying new techniques and applying different "rules" for a given painting or series of paintings because that grants me a greater sense of purpose and commitment to my work.

My aim is to make paintings that bring some feeling to the viewer that hopefully does not include repulsion.
