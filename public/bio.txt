I paint shape and color combinations that I enjoy looking at. I try to create pieces that give the viewer a sense of depth and perspective in a colorful environment. My work is inspired by nature, but not necessarily reflective of actual objects found in nature or the man-made world (because I am unable to do that). I view art as a means for people to experience the same subject, while each “seeing” different things and having different emotions. My goal is to create pieces that leave viewers with a positive feeling, or at least a feeling other than repulsion. I plan to continue painting for as long as I am allowed to do so.
